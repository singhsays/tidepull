Privacy policy

tidepull stores all data on your own machine. OAuth tokens and file metadata stay in a local database. Synced files stay in the local directories you configure.

tidepull has no server of its own and collects nothing. It communicates only with the cloud storage providers you configure in its config file, using the credentials you supply.

To request deletion of any data, remove the tidepull state database and config from your machine, or revoke the application's access in your cloud storage provider's account settings. For questions, contact singhsays@gmail.com.
